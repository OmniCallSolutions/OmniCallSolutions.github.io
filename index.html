PHONE NUMBER REQUIREMENTS

Business Phone Numbers & Regulatory Requirements

If your AI voice solution requires a new business phone number, the number may be subject to country-specific regulatory, verification and eligibility requirements before it can be provisioned.

Before requesting a business number

OmniCall Solutions can guide you through the number-selection and compliance process. Requirements vary according to the country, number type and telecommunications provider.

Local Numbers

Local business numbers may be available for eligible businesses, subject to the applicable country and provider requirements.

National Numbers

National numbers can provide a broader business presence where supported by the selected country and provider. Eligibility and documentation may apply.

Toll-Free & Special Numbers

Toll-free, premium, vanity or other special number types may have additional requirements or costs. If you require such a number, you may purchase it directly and we can assist you with the integration process.

View Regulatory Requirements

What you should be prepared for

  • Requirements vary by country and by phone-number type.
  • The telecommunications provider may need to verify the actual business or end user of the number.
  • Depending on the country and number type, information may include business name, business address, registration details, authorized representative information, identity documents or other supporting documents.
  • Some countries or number types may require a local address or other local eligibility conditions.
  • Additional regulatory documentation may be requested during the provider's review process.
  • Number availability and approval are determined by the telecommunications provider and applicable country rules.
  • Providing documents does not guarantee approval or availability of a particular number.

Important: Regulatory information can change. The information above is provided as general guidance and does not replace the current requirements shown by the phone-number provider for the selected country and number type.
